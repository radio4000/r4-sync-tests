# Radio4000

The web app live at [radio4000.com](https://radio4000.com).

## About R4

Radio4000 is a web-based platform to collect and play music.

[Start your radio](https://radio4000.com/auth/create-account?redirect=/create-channel) and fill it with tracks from media URLs like YouTube, Discogs and Soundcloud. Give each track a title, description, and a few #tags.

Search and filter your channel instantly, play it back, or broadcast live and let auto-radio keep every listener on the same moment. Control multiple decks at once, each with its own queue.

> Human curation over algorithms, since 2014.

Your tracks are yours, exportable anytime. Open API, open source, no ads, no tracking. The R4 CLI mirrors your radio offline.
